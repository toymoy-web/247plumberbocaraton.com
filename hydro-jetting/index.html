Hydro Jetting in Boca Raton, FL

If a drain has been snaked before and clogged again within a few weeks, or a line clogs on a regular schedule no matter what you avoid putting down it, hydro jetting clears the full pipe wall instead of punching a channel through the blockage.

What This Actually Is

High-pressure water, not a bigger snake

Most people calling about this don't say "hydro jetting," they describe a drain that keeps clogging, a kitchen line that's greasy no matter how many times it's snaked, or a main line that got stuck at 60 feet with a cable. Hydro jetting uses a hose that sprays high-pressure water in every direction as it moves through the pipe, scouring grease, scale, and root fragments off the full interior wall rather than just pushing a hole through the middle of the blockage.

Jetting vs. Snaking

Why snaking sometimes isn't enough

MethodWhat it doesBest for
Snaking/cablingPushes or grinds a path through the clogA single, isolated blockage
Hydro jettingScours the entire pipe diameter cleanGrease buildup, recurring clogs, root fragments

Florida's plumbing code, based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), doesn't specify a cleaning method, only a functioning drain, which is why the choice between jetting and snaking comes down to what will actually solve the recurring problem rather than a code requirement.

Where It's Used

Kitchen lines, main lines, and everything between

Kitchen drain lines that build up grease over years of cooking are one of the most common jetting jobs, since a snake alone tends to punch through the grease without removing it, letting the same buildup reform within weeks. Main sewer lines with root intrusion are another frequent case, particularly on older Boca Raton properties with clay or cast iron laterals where roots have worked into pipe joints over decades. In both situations, the goal is the same: leave the full interior surface of the pipe clean, not just a path through the middle of it.

Commercial kitchens see this even more than residential homes, since grease trap lines process far more volume and can go from clear to significantly restricted within just a few months without regular jetting maintenance. That's why we set up recurring jetting schedules for several local restaurants rather than waiting for a full backup each time.

Residential customers on a similar aging-line situation, particularly in Boca Raton's older neighborhoods, sometimes benefit from the same approach: a scheduled annual or biannual jetting pass on a line with known root activity, done proactively rather than reactively after each backup.

The equipment itself scales to the job: a smaller cart-mounted unit handles most residential interior lines, while a larger trailer-mounted jetter with higher pressure and flow is what a main sewer line or a commercial grease trap line typically calls for.

Whichever equipment is used, we walk the line's condition through with you afterward. That way you know whether the clog is genuinely resolved, or whether an underlying structural issue, like root intrusion at a cracked joint, is likely to bring the same problem back within a season.
