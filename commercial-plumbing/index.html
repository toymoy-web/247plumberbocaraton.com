What's Different Here

Commercial calls aren't just bigger residential jobs

Grease trap maintenance, backflow prevention testing, and multi-fixture restrooms are the plumbing realities of a commercial property, and each comes with its own code requirements that a residential-focused visit wouldn't cover. Florida's plumbing code, based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), sets stricter standards for commercial backflow prevention and grease interceptor sizing than for a single-family home, since a commercial failure has a larger public health impact if it goes wrong.

Property managers and business owners also need scheduling flexibility that residential customers usually don't: after-hours work to avoid disrupting a retail floor during business hours, or emergency response for a restaurant that can't operate without working plumbing during peak hours.

Boca Raton's mix of older strip retail centers along Federal Highway and newer Class A office space near Mizner Park and the Town Center corridor means we see a wide range of plumbing ages and configurations. That ranges from original 1980s fixture layouts to buildings on modern PEX systems installed within the last few years.

Compliance

Why commercial code requirements matter more here

A residential backflow issue is a homeowner's problem; a commercial backflow failure can contaminate a public water system, which is why Florida's plumbing code holds commercial properties to stricter testing and device requirements. Annual backflow testing is typically required for commercial properties, and letting that lapse can create liability beyond just a plumbing inconvenience. Grease interceptor sizing is similarly regulated for food service establishments, since an undersized or poorly maintained interceptor can send grease into the municipal sewer system.

We can also handle the documentation side of compliance, providing dated service records for backflow tests and grease trap pumping. That's what most municipalities and health inspectors expect to see on file, rather than paperwork you have to track down after the fact.
