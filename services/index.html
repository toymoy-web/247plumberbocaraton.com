What to do before we arrive during a water emergency

  • • Shut off the water at the main or the fixture's shutoff valve if it's safe and accessible to do so.
  • • Cut power to any outlets or fixtures near standing water rather than risking contact yourself.
  • • Move valuables and electronics away from the affected area if you can do so safely.
  • • Document the damage with photos before cleanup begins, useful for insurance purposes.
  • • Avoid disturbing visible mold growth; disturbing it can spread spores further into the home.
