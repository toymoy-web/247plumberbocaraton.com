  • • Are you licensed and insured? Yes, all work is performed by licensed plumbers, and we carry liability insurance covering our field work.
  • • Do you charge for estimates? A diagnostic visit typically carries a fee that's applied toward the repair if you move forward with the work; this is confirmed with you before a technician is dispatched.

How we handle scheduled versus emergency calls

Not every plumbing issue needs a middle-of-the-night dispatch, and we'll tell you honestly if your situation can reasonably wait for a scheduled appointment rather than paying for emergency response. Active leaks, sewage backups, no water at all, and anything actively causing property damage get priority dispatch regardless of the hour. A slow drain, a running toilet that isn't overflowing, or a fixture that's simply due for an upgrade can typically be scheduled during normal business hours at standard rates.

When you call, describe what's actually happening rather than what you think the fix is, that helps our dispatcher make an accurate priority call and get you the right response time.

Service area reminder

We dispatch throughout Boca Raton and the surrounding Palm Beach County towns listed on our areas we serve page. If you're just outside that coverage, it's still worth calling, we'll tell you plainly whether we can reach you within a reasonable window rather than taking the call and leaving you waiting.

Following up on completed work

If you have a question about work we've already completed, a warranty concern, or an invoice discrepancy, email is often the fastest route since it gives us a written record of your job details to reference. Include your service address and the approximate date of service so we can pull up the right record quickly.

What happens after you call

Our dispatcher takes down your address, a description of the issue, and any access details, then confirms an arrival window based on current technician availability and how urgent the situation is. For active emergencies, we prioritize dispatch and give you a realistic estimate rather than a vague "soon." For scheduled work, you'll get a specific appointment window, and we'll call ahead as the technician gets close so you're not left waiting without any update.

Once a technician arrives, they'll assess the situation, explain what they find in plain terms, and walk you through pricing before starting any billable repair beyond the initial diagnostic. You're never committed to a repair you haven't agreed to after hearing the actual scope and cost.

After the job is done

Once work is complete, you'll receive an invoice detailing what was done, parts used, and any applicable warranty on the labor or materials. Keep this documentation, it's useful both for your own records and if a warranty question comes up later. If anything about the completed work doesn't seem right, reach out promptly so we can address it while the job details are still fresh for everyone involved.
