Plumbing Blog

Practical, Boca Raton-specific answers to the plumbing questions we hear most, from permits and pipe age to what to do in an emergency before help arrives.

Most plumbing advice online is written for nowhere in particular. That means it skips the details that actually change what you should do here. A limestone aquifer leaves the water moderately hard. A slab-on-grade housing stock means a supply line leak has nowhere to go but up through the concrete. And permitting runs specifically through Palm Beach County and the City of Boca Raton under Florida's plumbing code (the IPC). These posts are written for this city specifically, based on the calls we actually get.

New posts get added as questions come up often enough to be worth writing about regularly, so check back if you don't see your specific situation covered here yet. If you don't find what you're looking for, calling and asking directly is always faster than searching.
