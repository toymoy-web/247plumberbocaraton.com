Why Descaling Matters Here

Florida's water and tankless heat exchangers

Tankless units heat water on demand by passing it through a compact heat exchanger, and mineral scale from Boca Raton's water supply builds up inside that exchanger faster than most homeowners expect, gradually restricting flow and reducing efficiency. Manufacturers generally recommend descaling once a year in areas with moderate to hard water, and skipping it is one of the most common reasons a tankless unit that should last 15-20 years starts showing problems well before that.

This is especially relevant in condo and townhome communities where a unit is often installed in a small utility closet with limited ventilation, since heat exchanger efficiency and venting both matter more in a tighter installation space than in an open garage setup.

Florida's plumbing code, based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), governs the installation and venting requirements for tankless units, and a unit that was installed correctly to code initially can still develop scale-related performance issues over time regardless of how well it was installed.

A whole-home water filtration or softening system can meaningfully slow scale buildup on a tankless unit, which is worth discussing if you're already dealing with frequent descaling needs or considering a new install in a Boca Raton home on municipal water.

Common Issues

What we see most often

Error codes

Most modern units display a specific error code that points directly to the cause, from ignition failure to a flow sensor issue, rather than requiring guesswork.

Inconsistent temperature

Often caused by a flow rate below the unit's minimum activation threshold, common when multiple fixtures run at once on an undersized unit.

Reduced hot water flow

Usually scale buildup restricting the heat exchanger, addressed with a descaling flush rather than a repair or replacement.

Before You Call

What to check first

  • • Note any error code displayed on the unit; it usually points directly to the cause.
  • • Mention roughly when the unit was last descaled, if you know.
  • • If multiple fixtures were running at once when the issue started, mention that too.

If the unit is completely unresponsive rather than showing an error, checking the gas supply valve and the electrical connection or battery (on units that use one for ignition) before calling can sometimes save a visit entirely.

The Descaling Process

What an annual service visit actually involves

A proper descaling flush isolates the unit using service valves, circulates a descaling solution through the heat exchanger for roughly 45 minutes to an hour, then flushes it clean with fresh water. We also check the inlet filter for debris, inspect the venting for any obstruction, and confirm the unit's error log is clear before calling the job done, rather than just running the flush and leaving.

For units under warranty, keeping a documented annual descaling record can also matter if a manufacturer defect claim ever comes up, since some warranties are conditioned on proof of regular maintenance rather than covering neglect-related failures.

We provide a dated service record after every descaling visit specifically for this reason, so you have documentation on hand if a warranty question ever arises down the road.
