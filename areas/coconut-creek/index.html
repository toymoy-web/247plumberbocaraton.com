Housing Mix

Condos, villages, and newer subdivisions

Coconut Creek has a mix of building types, from 55+ villages and condo communities that date back several decades to newer single-family subdivisions. In condo and village housing, a leak often starts inside a wall or ceiling shared with a neighbor, and the first step is working out whether the source is the unit or the building.

In older units, supply lines, shutoff valves, and water heaters are the usual suspects. Our leak detection work finds the source without opening more wall than necessary, which matters when the drywall belongs to someone else.

Before You Call

What to check first

  • • Is the leak inside the lines of your own unit, or does the water appear to come from a wall or ceiling you share?

  • • When were the shutoff valves in your unit last exercised or replaced?

Permits and Jurisdiction

Broward County, with association rules on top

Coconut Creek is in Broward County, so permit questions go to the local Broward building department rather than Palm Beach County. In communities with an association, you may also need management approval for work that touches shared lines.

Working in Associations
