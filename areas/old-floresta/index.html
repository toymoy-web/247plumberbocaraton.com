Neighborhood Background

About Old Floresta

Old Floresta is a residential neighborhood in Boca Raton conceived by architect Addison Mizner in the 1920s. The Florida Division of Historical Resources marker at West Palmetto Park Road and Cardinal Avenue describes Spanish-style houses with rough-finished stucco walls, wrought iron balconies, and barrel tile roofs on narrow, tree-lined streets. The marker credits Mizner with designing 29 homes here.

Old Floresta became Boca Raton's first historic district in 1990. The Fred C. Aiken House, designed in 1925 and built in 1926, was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1992, and it is one of two Boca Raton houses on the Register that sit in Old Floresta.

Historic District Status

Check with the city before exterior work

The City of Boca Raton explains that owners in a locally designated historic district generally need a Certificate of Appropriateness before changing the outside of a building. Most plumbing repairs happen inside the walls and under the slab, but if a job could touch the exterior, such as new vents, exterior equipment, or access through a masonry wall, ask the city first. City Planning and Zoning can be reached at (561) 393-7750.

It also helps to know what kind of home you have. Houses designed by Mizner and later alterations can sit side by side on the same street, so a quick description of the address on the phone gives us a better picture than the neighborhood name alone.
