Neighborhood Background

About Pearl City

Pearl City is a historic African-American neighborhood and is described as the oldest community in Boca Raton. It was established in 1915 as housing for agricultural workers, and it sits directly south of Glades Road between Dixie Highway and Federal Highway.

Boca Raton's City Council designated Pearl City a local historic district in 2002. The National Park Service listed the Pearl City National Register Historic District on December 7, 2023. A Martin Luther King Jr. memorial stands in a small park on the grounds of Ebenezer Baptist Church at the northeast corner of the neighborhood.

Historic District Status

Check with the city before exterior work

Because Pearl City is a locally designated historic district, the City of Boca Raton's general rule applies: owners usually need a Certificate of Appropriateness before changing the outside of a building. Typical repairs such as a leak behind a wall or a drain clog happen inside the home. If a job could affect the exterior, confirm with City Planning and Zoning at (561) 393-7750 before work starts.
