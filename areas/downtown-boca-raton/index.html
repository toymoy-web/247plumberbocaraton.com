Neighborhood Background

About downtown and Mizner Park

Mizner Park opened on January 11, 1991, on the site of the former Boca Mall, an enclosed shopping center that opened in 1974. It is a Mediterranean Revival style district of luxury apartments, shops, and restaurants at 327 Plaza Real, Boca Raton, FL 33432.

Anchors include the Boca Raton Museum of Art at the north end and the Mizner Park Amphitheater, an open-air venue the City of Boca Raton operates for concerts and festivals.

Mixed-Use Properties

When homes and businesses share a block

A mixed-use district puts residents, shops, restaurants, and offices close together. A leak in one space can show up in the space next to it, so it helps to know who manages the building and where the nearest shutoff valve is. For a rented unit or tenant space, tell your property manager or landlord about the leak as well as calling us.

We handle both residential and commercial calls. For businesses, our commercial plumbing page lists what we work on, and we try to schedule around business hours whenever the problem allows.
