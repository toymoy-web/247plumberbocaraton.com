Historic Homes

What an original 1920s Osceola Park bungalow usually needs

Homes from Delray's original 1920s building boom were plumbed long before modern code, often with galvanized steel supply lines that corrode from the inside. Water flow gradually restricts, sometimes for decades before anyone notices beyond "the pressure's always been like this." A full or partial repipe to PEX or copper is the standard fix, and Florida's plumbing code governs the permit and inspection either way.

If your home is in the Marina Historic District or another designated historic area, exterior work can also involve additional review beyond the standard plumbing permit, which we factor into scheduling on those addresses.
